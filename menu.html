Dietary Notes

Our veggie taco, rice and beans and chips are vegetarian. Ask for no crema to make them vegan. All of our tacos can be served on corn tortillas, which are naturally gluten-free, but our kitchen is not a certified gluten-free space.

Spice Level Guide

  • Mild: pico de gallo and lime crema
  • Medium: roasted tomato salsa
  • Hot: salsa roja with chile de árbol
  • Extra hot: habanero salsa, ask at the window if you dare
