Our Story

Golden Hour Tacos started when two friends decided that the best part of the day, the golden hour before sunset, deserved better food. We bought an old step van, fixed it up in a driveway and served our first tacos at a local farmers market. Today we are a small family-run truck that parks outside breweries, markets and office parks around Orange County.

What We Cook With

  • Corn tortillas pressed fresh every morning
  • Steak and carnitas from a local butcher
  • Produce from nearby farms whenever it is in season
  • Salsas and crema made from scratch each day

Meet the Team

Maria runs the grill and builds the menu. Dan handles the salsas, the schedule and catering. Between them they have more than twenty years of kitchen experience, and they are usually the ones handing you your order.
